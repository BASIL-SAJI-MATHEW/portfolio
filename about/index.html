About Basil Saji Mathew

Basil Saji Mathew (also written Basil Saji) is a Lua and FiveM developer and a backend and security automation engineer. He works between Bengaluru, India and Abu Dhabi, UAE, and remotely, and speaks English and Hindi. He is the founder of Altivora Hosting.

What he does

  • Builds Lua and FiveM resources for ESX, QBCore and Qbox servers.
  • Builds backend APIs and automation with Node.js, Redis, PostgreSQL and MySQL.
  • Builds security tools that detect and block brute-force attacks on Windows RDP and Linux SSH.
  • Runs Altivora Hosting: NVMe VPS, game server, Discord bot and database hosting with DDoS protection in India, Singapore, the USA, Germany and Canada.
